Elevator Radio

A little escape, one floor away. Instrumental music created on your iPhone.

Support

For help, contact info@nauralproject.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Using the radio

Open Lobby and choose a floor to start. Pause or resume below the cabin. Choose New mood for a fresh musical starting point, or use the heart to save a mood.

Settings includes the sleep timer, reduced motion and Stop radio. Saved moods are stored locally. Clear saved moods in Settings removes them from the app.

Sometimes the elevator gets stuck. Keep riding returns to the Lobby; opening the author's links is optional. Playback controls remain available.
