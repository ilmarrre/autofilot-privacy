Elevator Radio privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026. Publisher: NAURAL PROJECT LTD.

Music and data collection

Elevator Radio creates instrumental music on your device. This version has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDK, microphone recording or cloud music-generation service. It does not send listening history or saved moods to a developer server.

Local storage

The app stores saved station and seed moods, volume, selected floor and reduced-motion preferences in private local app storage. You can remove saved moods in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to iOS backup and restoration behavior. Saved-mood files are marked for exclusion from backup; the app does not control the operating system's backup behavior for other preferences.

Apple platform services

Music playback, system media controls, audio interruptions and elapsed-time sleep timers use Apple's platform services. Local preference and elapsed-time values are not used for fingerprinting or remote analytics.

External websites

Author and original-elevator links open external websites only when you choose them. Those sites have their own privacy practices. The app does not silently contact those sites to generate or play music.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process network information when serving web pages according to GitHub's privacy statement. This is separate from the app's local music generation.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact NAURAL PROJECT LTD. at info@nauralproject.com.
