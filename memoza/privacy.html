Memoza privacy policy · Effective 14 October 2026
Publisher: NAURAL PROJECT LTD.

What you choose to save

Memoza receives words, phrases and passages when you explicitly share, paste or type them. It keeps the original text, any supplied source information, translations, examples and your practice history. It does not read other apps, messages or browsing activity in the background. It has no separate Memoza account, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK.

Storage and private iCloud sync

Your collection is stored locally on your iPhone or Mac. When iCloud is available, vocabulary, original captures, edits, examples and practice history synchronize through the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Vocabulary and pending capture payloads use CloudKit encrypted fields. The publisher cannot browse the contents of users’ private databases. Memoza does not operate a separate server for your library.

Sync requires a working iCloud account and available storage. Offline changes wait until a later successful sync. Devices must use the same Apple Account. Apple provides iCloud and handles its own service data under Apple’s privacy policy. Memoza stores a local account identifier to avoid sending one account’s library into another account.

Language assistance

Automatic translation and language assistance use Apple frameworks and supported on-device models. Availability depends on the device, operating system, region, languages and downloaded resources. This version does not send your text to a cloud AI service. You can edit meanings and examples or enter them manually. Generated suggestions may be inaccurate.

Optional system features

Pronunciation uses system speech synthesis. Optional reminders use local notifications. The iPhone “Before apps” feature runs only through a personal automation you create in Apple Shortcuts. Memoza cannot create or inspect your automations and does not monitor app use in the background. Opening another app or an external help page is an explicit action.

Export, deletion and retention

You can export a collection backup from iPhone Settings. The file contains your personal vocabulary and learning history; its storage and sharing are under your control. You can delete a word from its details. On iPhone, Settings → Privacy & your data also provides a collection deletion action. Edits and deletions synchronize to connected devices when sync succeeds.

Deletion removes the word’s content from the active library. Minimal identifiers and deletion markers may remain to prevent an offline device from restoring deleted words; these do not retain the deleted word’s original text. Pending share captures are retained until successfully imported. Copies you exported, operating-system backups and data on offline devices are not immediately removed by deleting the active collection. Removing the app does not by itself delete its iCloud library.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use them to respond to your request. Please avoid sending passwords, verification codes or private vocabulary unless needed to explain an issue. You can ask about deleting support correspondence by contacting us.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and contain no analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub may process network information when serving the pages under GitHub’s privacy statement. This website is separate from the app’s private library.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact NAURAL PROJECT LTD. at info@nauralproject.com. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
