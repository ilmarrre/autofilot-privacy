Save a word

On iPhone or Mac, select text in an app that supports sharing, then choose Share → Memoza. If Memoza is missing, enable it in the sharing extensions. Some apps share a URL rather than the selected text; copy and paste the passage into Memoza in that case. You can also add a word or phrase manually.

Shared text is saved before enrichment. If translation or language assistance is unavailable, your capture stays saved: open its details to add or edit a meaning.

A quick card before another app

On iPhone with iOS 18.2 or later, enable Settings → Before apps → Learn before apps. In Apple Shortcuts, create a personal App Is Opened automation, choose Run Immediately, add Memoza’s “Learn before apps” action, and select where to return.

A card appears only when a word is eligible and the cooldown and daily limit allow it. You can always skip. Memoza does not block other apps. You may see a brief app switch or a Shortcuts notification. If return to the selected app is unavailable, use the app switcher. This automation is an iPhone feature; it is not part of the Mac app.

To see a particular word without changing its learning history or review date, open the word on iPhone and choose “Preview a quick practice” from its menu. Regular practice remains available on iOS 17 and on Mac.

Sync between iPhone and Mac

Use the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled on both devices. Check the iCloud sync status in Memoza Settings. If changes have not arrived, check your connection and available iCloud storage, then choose Sync now. Offline changes need a later successful sync. No separate Memoza account is required.

Translations and pronunciation

Apple Translation and on-device language assistance depend on your device, system version, languages and downloaded resources. They are not guaranteed for every language pair. You can always enter and correct a meaning manually. The app does not send text to a cloud AI service.

Your collection

Delete individual words from their details. On iPhone, Settings contains backup export and collection deletion. Deletions sync to other connected devices; exported files are your own copies. Read the privacy policy for details.
