Autofilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Autofilot is a macOS file organisation app provided by NAURAL PROJECT LTD., the controller for the information described here. Contact us at info@nauralproject.com. Our company website's policy separately covers visits to that website.

Local organisation

You choose the folders Autofilot can access using macOS folder pickers. Local scanning reads file names, locations, metadata, supported document text and text recognised in images. It reads examples from connected project folders to suggest destinations. Local organisation and OCR run on your Mac without sending document content to us.

The app stores folder permissions, settings, extracted text, file records, project context, learned corrections, suggestions and move history on your Mac. This supports search, organisation and undo. Autofilot has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, and does not sell personal information or use it for cross-app tracking.

Optional deep analysis

When available with your subscription, deep analysis is a separate action. You select files and explicitly agree to share their excerpts and previews for that batch. Scanning a folder or enabling local automatic sorting does not itself enable deep analysis. You review AI suggestions before moving files.

For each selected file, the app can send its name, up to 5,000 bytes of extracted text, up to two reduced JPEG page or image previews, and up to 32 candidate folder labels and their identifiers. Names are bounded to 180 bytes and folder labels to 240 bytes. Previews are re-encoded with metadata removed and limited to 256 KB each. These fields may reveal personal or confidential information. Full filesystem paths, original document files, unselected documents, and the content of learned project examples are not sent by this feature. A hash representing the current project context is included.

Requests go over HTTPS to the Autofilot service operated by NAURAL PROJECT LTD. on Google Cloud / Firebase. Our service sends the selected content and candidate labels to OpenAI's API to generate a suggestion. Apple verifies subscription status. These providers receive the information necessary for those functions, and infrastructure providers may receive connection and security information such as IP addresses and request times.

Our service does not deliberately persist document excerpts, preview images or names in its database or application logs. It keeps a pseudonymous subscription identifier, request hashes, usage and cost counters, request status, and a limited response containing folder identifiers, confidence and fixed explanation labels. Results are available for recovery for up to 24 hours; background expiry cleanup can take longer. Usage and request records are kept while needed to enforce subscription limits, prevent repeated charges, investigate incidents and satisfy legal obligations. A failed or interrupted request may retain a reservation where provider usage cannot be established safely.

OpenAI does not use API data to train models by default. Its abuse-monitoring retention is generally up to 30 days; legal and safety exceptions may apply. We request no stored Responses API application state, but that does not eliminate all provider retention. Prompt caching can retain encrypted representations for up to 24 hours. See OpenAI's API data controls for the current details.

Our service is configured for London hosting. Providers and support systems may process information in other countries, including the United States. Where required, contractual transfer protections must apply. Deep analysis is enabled only after the relevant service and provider arrangements are in place. You may stop future submissions by not starting another analysis; pausing stops subsequent files, but cannot recall a request already sent.

Purchases and access

Apple handles payments; we do not receive payment card details. The app uses StoreKit. For hosted analysis, it sends a signed Apple transaction to our service, which verifies the subscription with Apple. Subscription identifiers are transformed into keyed hashes for stored usage records. Such identifiers are pseudonymous, not anonymous. The app requires no separate Autofilot account.

Local records and deletion

Local data remains on your Mac until removed. Deep-analysis suggestions and the remaining file selection are saved locally. An interrupted request can also save its bounded excerpt and previews locally so you can explicitly recover the same request. Removing a connected folder stops future access but does not necessarily remove old records, learned corrections or history, and does not undo moves.

To erase local records, quit Autofilot and remove its application-support data from its macOS app container. This removes settings and undo history, not your original documents. Uninstalling alone may leave local records behind. Contact support for help locating the correct container. Backups follow your backup settings. Development versions that allow a custom provider store that provider's key in macOS Keychain; it must be removed separately. Custom-provider processing follows the provider you select and is not offered in the App Store build.

Support

If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever name, message, screenshots or files you provide. We use this to answer the request and resolve problems through our email and infrastructure providers. Please use sample files or redacted screenshots and do not send passwords or API keys. Support records are retained while needed for the request, follow-up, security, disputes and legal obligations. We may disclose information when lawfully required or to establish or defend legal claims.

Legal bases and your rights

Where UK or European data-protection law applies, the basis for optional content analysis is your consent. Subscription verification and requested service delivery are necessary to perform our contract. Abuse prevention and service security rely on our legitimate interests in protecting the service. Support relies on providing requested assistance and our legitimate interests in resolving issues. Legal obligations can require retention or disclosure.

You can withdraw consent for future analysis without affecting prior lawful processing. Depending on applicable law and circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure, portability or restriction of your information. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact info@nauralproject.com; we may need proportionate information to locate pseudonymous records and verify the request. We cannot remotely erase information held only on your Mac. We will explain any information that must be retained.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or your local data-protection authority.

Security and policy changes

Autofilot uses macOS sandbox permissions and HTTPS. Your Mac's account and storage protections protect local records; the app adds no separate encryption layer to that database. Hosted credentials stay on the server. No security measure removes every risk. Material changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy and the in-app disclosure before new optional processing is enabled.
