Autofilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Autofilot is a macOS file organisation app provided by NAURAL PROJECT LTD. This policy explains how the app handles information and how we handle information you send us for support. Our company website's privacy policy separately covers visits to that website.

Files are processed on your Mac by default

You choose the folders Autofilot can access through macOS folder pickers. The app can read file names, locations, metadata, supported document text and text recognised in images. It analyses selected destination folders and examples to suggest where files belong. Local classification, text extraction and OCR run on your Mac. In local mode, the app does not send your files or extracted text to NAURAL PROJECT LTD.

Autofilot stores folder permissions, settings, file records, extracted text or excerpts, folder context, learned corrections, suggestions and action history locally. History can include original and destination paths and explanations for moves and renames. This information supports organisation, search, learning and undo. The app has no publisher account system, advertising SDK or built-in third-party analytics SDK.

Optional cloud suggestions

Cloud classification is off by default. You can choose to configure an HTTPS AI endpoint, model and your own API key, and consent before enabling it. When enabled, low-confidence classifications may be sent directly from your Mac to that endpoint. NAURAL PROJECT LTD. does not operate a relay for these requests.

A request can include the file name (up to 180 UTF-8 bytes), an extracted-text excerpt (up to 5,000 bytes), and up to 32 candidate folders, each with a local identifier and display name (up to 120 bytes). These fields may contain personal or confidential information. The request also includes the model, classification instructions and your API key for authentication. Original file bytes, full filesystem paths, source URLs and learned document examples are not included by the cloud-classification client. The provider also receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address.

Your chosen provider's terms and privacy policy govern its processing, retention, training use and processing locations. Providers may process information outside your country. Review those policies and ensure you are authorised to share the relevant content before enabling cloud suggestions. You can switch back to local processing in Settings. This stops future cloud classifications but does not delete information already received by a provider; contact that provider for deletion requests. The saved API key is stored in macOS Keychain. Disabling cloud processing does not itself erase the saved key.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments and subscriptions under its own privacy policy. Autofilot uses StoreKit on your device to obtain product information and verify subscription access. We do not receive your payment card details through the app and do not operate a receipt-validation server for Autofilot. Apple may separately provide us with developer sales, financial or diagnostic reports under your Apple settings and its terms.

Support information

If you contact us, we receive the email address, name, message and any files or diagnostic details you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate problems and maintain records of the request. Please avoid sending passwords, API keys or confidential documents; redact screenshots and use sample files where possible. Support communications may be processed by the email and infrastructure services we use to provide support, and information may be disclosed when required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We do not sell support information or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention and your controls

Local app records remain on your Mac until removed. Removing a watched or destination folder in Settings removes that access from Autofilot and stops its future use, but does not necessarily erase existing file records, learned information or history. It does not undo previous file moves. The app does not offer a complete in-app data-erasure control in this version.

To erase local app records, quit Autofilot and remove its application-support data from its macOS app container. This deletes settings and undo history, not your original documents. Uninstalling the app alone may leave app data or Keychain entries behind. Remove the saved Autofilot cloud API credential separately through macOS Keychain Access if desired. Contact us for help locating the correct data for your installed version. Copies in your backups remain subject to your backup settings.

We retain support information only for as long as needed to resolve the request and any related follow-up, security, legal or dispute obligations. The appropriate period depends on the request and applicable obligations. You can ask us to delete information we hold; where we must retain some information, we will explain why. We cannot remotely erase data held only on your Mac or by a provider you select.

Legal bases and privacy rights

Where UK or European data-protection law applies, we process support information as necessary to provide requested support or perform our contract with you, for our legitimate interests in resolving problems and protecting our services, and to meet applicable legal obligations. Optional cloud processing is initiated through your explicit choice and consent in the app; your independently selected provider is responsible for explaining its own legal basis.

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or obtain a copy of your personal information, restrict its use, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it without affecting prior lawful processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or your local data-protection authority.

Security and changes

Autofilot uses macOS sandbox folder permissions, Keychain storage for the cloud API key, and HTTPS for optional cloud requests. Local app records are protected by your Mac's account and storage protections; the app does not add its own encryption layer to those records. No security measure eliminates every risk. We update this policy when the product or our handling of information changes and identify the revision date above.
